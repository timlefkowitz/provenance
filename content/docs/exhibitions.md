# Exhibitions

Plan, manage, and publish exhibitions on Provenance — from solo shows to multi-artist group exhibitions.

## Create an exhibition

1. Go to **Exhibitions** (`/exhibitions`) from the Toolbox.
2. Click **New exhibition**.
3. Fill in title, dates, venue, description, and cover image.
4. Save as a draft.

## Inviting artists

For group shows, invite artists to submit work:

1. Open the exhibition editor.
2. Generate **artist invites** with unique tokens.
3. Share the invite link — artists land on `/exhibitions/submit?token=…`.
4. Review submissions and accept or decline artworks.

## Publishing

When ready, **publish** the exhibition. Published exhibitions:

- Appear on your creator website at `/{handle}/exhibitions/[id]`
- Can be featured in open calls and portal activity
- Include a public page with artwork list and details

## Editing & unpublishing

Edit exhibition details anytime at `/exhibitions/[id]/edit`. Unpublishing removes the public page but preserves all data and submissions.

## Exhibition on your site

Published exhibitions automatically sync to your creator website. The site template renders exhibition pages with artwork grids, dates, and venue information.

## Subscription

Exhibition management is available to all authenticated users. Advanced operations (loans tied to exhibitions, insurance valuations) are in the [Operations](/docs/operations) module.
