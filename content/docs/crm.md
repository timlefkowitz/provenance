# CRM

The CRM (Customer Relationship Management) module helps artists and galleries track collectors, leads, and outreach — branded as **Opportunities & Relationships**.

## Access

Navigate to **CRM** (`/portal/or`) from the Toolbox. Access requires:

- Artist account (or gallery team member with CRM permission)
- Active subscription

## Kanban board

The CRM uses a kanban-style board with stages:

| Stage | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **Lead** | New contacts and initial interest |
| **Contacted** | Outreach sent, awaiting response |
| **Engaged** | Active conversation or relationship |
| **Collector** | Confirmed collector or buyer |
| **Archived** | Closed or inactive |

Drag cards between stages to update status.

## Adding leads

Create leads manually from the CRM panel:

1. Click **Add lead**.
2. Enter name, email, notes, and source.
3. The lead appears in the **Lead** column.

Leads can also originate from site contact forms and certificate interactions.

## Lead details

Click any card to view and edit:

- Contact information
- Notes and activity history
- Linked artworks or exhibitions
- Tags and priority

## CRM team access

Gallery owners can grant CRM access to team members. CRM data is scoped to the artist or gallery profile you're operating under.

## Mailing list integration

CRM contacts sync with the [Mailing list](/docs/mailing-list) module. Export contacts for email campaigns or manage them from `/mailing-list`.

## Search

Use the contact search API (in-app) to find existing CRM contacts when composing emails or linking to artworks.
