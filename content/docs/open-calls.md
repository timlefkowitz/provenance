# Open calls

Open calls are opportunities for artists to submit work to exhibitions, residencies, and curatorial projects posted on Provenance.

## Browse open calls

Visit `/open-calls` or `/open-calls/browse` to see active calls. Each listing shows:

- Host organization or curator
- Deadline
- Medium requirements
- Submission guidelines

## Viewing a call

Click any open call to see full details at `/open-calls/[slug]`, including eligibility, required materials, and the submission form.

## Submitting

1. Open the call detail page.
2. Click **Submit**.
3. Select artworks from your collection or upload new work.
4. Add a statement or cover letter if required.
5. Submit before the deadline.

You'll receive confirmation and can track submission status from the **Portal** dashboard.

## Creating open calls

Galleries and curators can post open calls:

1. Go to `/open-calls` and create a new call.
2. Set title, deadline, description, and submission requirements.
3. Publish — the call appears in browse listings.

## AI opportunities assistant

With an active subscription, artists can use the **Opportunities** AI chat to discover open calls matched to their practice. The assistant searches active calls and recommends relevant opportunities.

Access it from the grants/opportunities area or via `/api/opportunities/chat` (in-app only).
