# Mailing list

Manage contacts and send email outreach to collectors, curators, and your network.

## Access

Go to **Mailing List** (`/mailing-list`) from the Toolbox. Available to artists and gallery team members with appropriate permissions.

## Contacts

The mailing list shows contacts from your CRM (`artist_leads` table). Each contact includes:

- Name and email
- Source (manual, site form, import)
- Tags and notes
- Last contacted date

## Adding contacts

Add contacts manually or import from CRM leads. Contacts created in the CRM automatically appear here.

## Email outreach

Compose and send emails to contacts directly from the mailing list panel. Emails are sent via Provenance's transactional email system (Resend).

> **Note:** Respect CAN-SPAM and GDPR requirements. Only email contacts who have opted in or have an existing relationship with you.

## Integration with CRM

The mailing list and CRM share the same contact database. Changes in one reflect in the other:

- Move a lead to **Engaged** in CRM → still available in mailing list
- Add a note in mailing list → visible in CRM card details

## Best practices

- Segment contacts by interest (collectors, curators, press).
- Track last contacted date to avoid over-emailing.
- Link outreach to specific exhibitions or artworks for context.
