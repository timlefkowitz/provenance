# Getting started

Welcome to **Provenance** — a platform for preserving cultural heritage through artwork authentication, provenance records, and professional tools for artists, collectors, and galleries.

## Create an account

1. Visit [provenance.guru](https://provenance.guru) and click **Sign up**.
2. Register with email/password or continue with Google.
3. Complete **onboarding** to choose your role: **Artist**, **Collector**, or **Gallery**.

New accounts receive a **14-day trial** with access to subscription features.

## Choose your role

| Role | Best for |
|------|----------|
| **Artist** | Documenting your work, issuing certificates, applying to grants and open calls |
| **Collector** | Cataloging a collection, tracking provenance, requesting research |
| **Gallery** | Managing artists, exhibitions, CRM, and gallery operations |

You can switch context using the **profile switcher** in the navigation bar when you have multiple profiles.

## Main navigation

After signing in, you'll see:

- **Artworks** — Browse verified public artworks or add to your collection
- **Artists** — The artist and gallery registry
- **Collection** — Your personal artwork records (`/artworks/my`)
- **Portal** — Dashboard with favorites, activity, and quick links
- **Toolbox** — Website editor, exhibitions, grants, CRM, mailing list, and operations

## Your first steps

### As an artist

1. Create a [public profile](/docs/artists-and-profiles).
2. [Add your first artwork](/docs/artworks-and-collection).
3. Fill in [provenance details](/docs/certificates-and-provenance) and issue a certificate.
4. [Publish your creator website](/docs/creator-websites).

### As a collector

1. Add artworks to your collection.
2. Request provenance research from the certificate page.
3. Browse the public registry to discover artists.

### As a gallery

1. Set up your gallery profile and invite team members.
2. Create an [exhibition](/docs/exhibitions) and invite artists.
3. Use the [CRM](/docs/crm) to track collectors and leads.
